# RpTv privacy policy

*Last updated: October 14, 2026*

RpTv is a free, open-source IPTV player for iPhone and iPad. It doesn't collect, store or share any personal
data. There are no accounts, analytics, ads, tracking or crash reporting.

## What stays on your device

Everything you enter or that RpTv downloads stays on your device:

- The playlists and Xtream Codes accounts you add, and their channels, movies, series and TV guides.
- Your favorites, recently watched items, watch progress, category changes and settings.
- Xtream Codes passwords and the parental controls PIN, which are kept in the iOS keychain.

None of it is sent to the developer or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it. Sources you export to a file
go wherever you send that file, without their passwords.

## Network connections

RpTv connects only to the servers you give it: the addresses of your playlists, Xtream Codes accounts and TV
guides, and the stream, logo and poster addresses they list. Those servers see your IP address and the requests
your device makes, as with any app that plays online media. Their own privacy policies apply. RpTv doesn't
contact any server of its own.

When you use AirPlay with a stream the TV can't play directly, RpTv converts it on your device and serves it to
the TV over your local network only.

Trailer buttons open the trailer's address (usually YouTube) in your browser or its app.

## Permissions

- **Local network:** to reach IPTV servers on your home network and to send converted streams to AirPlay
  devices.
- **Face ID or Touch ID** (optional): to open categories locked by parental controls. RpTv only receives
  whether the check passed.
- **Notifications** (optional): for programme reminders you set in the TV guide. They are scheduled on your
  device.

## Children

RpTv isn't directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
